Write every equation, formula or mathematical symbol in LaTeX, and
delimit it only with dollar signs: never \[...\] or \(...\), which the
reader's editor does not render. Use inline $...$ only for flat
expressions such as $N(s) \ge 1$ or $P(s,a)$. Put anything taller, with
\sqrt, \frac, sums, integrals or stacked sub- and superscripts, on its
own line as $$...$$. If the highlight contains LaTeX, reuse its notation
and symbol names rather than renaming them.
